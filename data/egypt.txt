Egypt Travel Guide

Egypt is known for its ancient history, archaeological sites, museums, beaches, and Nile River.

Cairo:
Cairo is the capital of Egypt and a major starting point for travelers. Important attractions include the Giza Pyramids, the Great Sphinx, the Egyptian Museum, and historic areas of Islamic Cairo.

Giza:
The Giza Plateau contains the Great Pyramid of Giza, the Pyramid of Khafre, the Pyramid of Menkaure, and the Great Sphinx.

Luxor:
Luxor is known for ancient Egyptian archaeological sites. Important attractions include Karnak Temple, Luxor Temple, the Valley of the Kings, and the Valley of the Queens.

Aswan:
Aswan is located in southern Egypt along the Nile River. Visitors can explore the Nile, Philae Temple, and nearby Nubian villages.

Red Sea:
Egypt's Red Sea destinations are known for beaches, diving, snorkeling, and marine life. Popular destinations include Hurghada and Sharm El-Sheikh.

Transportation:
Travelers can use domestic flights, trains, buses, taxis, and private transportation to move between Egyptian destinations.

Travel Planning:
A first-time visitor interested in ancient history may consider combining Cairo with Luxor. Travelers interested in beaches and water activities can consider Red Sea destinations.
